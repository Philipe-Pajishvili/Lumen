From movement to voice

Five steps, start to finish

Every conversation with Lumen follows the same simple path. No settings to fight with mid‑sentence — just watch, move, and Lumen speaks for you.

  1. 01

    Press the Voice button

    Activates Lumen's voice communication mode.

  2. 02

    Wait for the highlighted word

    A selector moves across the board until it reaches the word you want to say.

  3. 03

    Use your trained limb

    Perform the movement Lumen was trained to recognize for you.

  4. 04

    Check the input display

    The indicator at the top of the screen confirms your movement was recognized.

  5. 05

    Lumen speaks the word

    Once detected, Lumen announces the selected word aloud.
