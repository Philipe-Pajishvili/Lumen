A gesture based experience that turns pose recognition into spoken words for more natural communication.

Real-time gesture detection Voice playback Communication board

What this app does

Use simple poses to select phrases, speak them aloud, and support everyday communication with a clear visual board.

  • Recognizes hand and body poses
  • Speaks selected phrases with voice output
  • Lets users browse a communication board quickly
